# War Room

**Build an AI decision-rehearsal agent in Microsoft Copilot Studio.**

War Room puts you inside real Indian business decisions on the day they were made, before you know how they ended. You decide, you defend your decision against stakeholders, and only then do you find out what really happened and what it teaches.

---

## ⬇️ Download the kit

**Option 1 (recommended):** open **Releases** on the right side of this page and download `WarRoom_Student_Kit.zip`.

**Option 2:** click the green **Code** button at the top of this page, then **Download ZIP**.

After downloading, **extract (unzip) the file** before you start. Copilot Studio can't read files that are still inside a zip.

---

## 📦 What's in the kit

| File | What it is | When you use it |
|---|---|---|
| `WarRoom_Build_Sheet.pdf` | Your step-by-step guide with checkpoints | Open it first and keep it open |
| `WarRoom_CasePack_SEALED.pdf` | Five real Indian business cases (the agent's knowledge) | Stage 2: upload it to your agent |
| `1_Instructions.txt` | The agent's instructions and Integrity Protocol | Stage 3: paste into your agent |
| `2_Skills/` | Four skills, as `.zip` files | Stage 4: upload in order, as zips |
| `WarRoom_RedTeam_Protocol.pdf` | How to test your neighbour's agent | Stage 7: red-team round |

> ⚠️ **Do not read Section 2 of the Case Pack.** It contains the sealed outcomes. Reading it yourself is like reading the last page of a thriller first.

---

## ✅ Ground rules

- **Driver** types, **Navigator** reads and checks. Swap seats at Stage 5.
- Change only the **PERSONALITY** line in the instructions.
- Don't skip a checkpoint. Fix it before moving on.
- Start a **fresh Preview conversation** after any change.
- Play at most **two full cases** plus the red-team round. Every test uses shared credits.

---

*The cases are compiled from public reporting for educational use. Sources are listed in the Case Pack.*
